# B6 and B7 findings: column properties, business units and role privileges (6 Nov 2026)

Read-only (GET only). UAT 22:25 to 22:32 IST (943 requests), production about 22:32 to 22:50 IST (903 requests). Data saved locally only.

## B6: properties of customer columns
6,368 columns exist in both environments (UAT 6,443, production 6,377). Only 22 differ in a property:
| Property | Differ | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Maximum length | 17 | dg_servicecall.dg_servicecallname UAT 400, PROD 250 (also on 3 related tables); dg_quoteproductname UAT 1000, PROD 850 (3 tables); dg_customeraddress.dg_address1street2 UAT 40, PROD 54; dg_importdata.dg_consigneeadd1 UAT 100, PROD 126; dg_routemaster zones UAT 10, PROD 11 |
| Precision (money) | 2 | salesorderdetail.dg_commissioncurrency and its _base: UAT precision 4 with precision source 2, PROD precision 2 with source 0 |
| Required level | 3 | dg_quantitydiscountmaster.dg_division (required in UAT only), productpricelevel.dg_name (required in PROD only), quote.dg_contact (required in PROD only) |
| Data type, minimum, maximum | 0 | |
Custom column counts: quotedetail UAT 531, PROD 534; salesorderdetail UAT 505, PROD 506.
Meaning: the column definitions are very close. The money precision difference on salesorderdetail.dg_commissioncurrency matters for the Currency Field Assessment (do not treat UAT as the reference for precision).

## B7: business units
UAT 32, production 33; only 9 names in both. UAT has the Phenolix and Solvent sales district units (for example "North Sales District (Z00001) - NE Phenolix") plus [Test] and [Deprecated] units. Production has the DPL units (for example "North_Phenolix_DPL", "Export_Solvents_DPL") and the department units without the [Test] prefix. The root unit is named after the environment (deepakgroupuat, deepakcrmprod).
Effect: the Restrict Views plugin filters by sales organisation and division through business unit names. In UAT it would not behave as in production, so UAT cannot be used to test it.

## B7: role privileges
Roots roles: UAT 347, PROD 315; 313 in both; 34 only in UAT, 2 only in PROD (Agentic Simulation User, Transformation). 68 roles in both have different privilege sets.
Limit: the privilege list is capped at 5,000 per role, so the largest roles (System Administrator, System Customizer, Service Writer, System Admin-Deepak) are truncated and their differences are not reliable. For the others the differences are real, for example Deepak Admin (UAT 1,161 against PROD 991), CEO (1,089 against 941), CFO (1,086 against 949), Product Head, Marketing HOD, MDM User, Finance Team Member. UAT mostly holds more privileges, partly because it has more tables.

## Effect on D01
Column definitions are close; business units and roles are not. A UAT-based environment needs its business unit structure and role privileges aligned to production before the Restrict Views plugin, security and performance work can be tested there.
